# Meal Planner - Sources and Research

## Online Sources Consulted
1. IIT Delhi Hostel Mess Menus (Aravali, Kailash, Saptagiri) - Found on Scribd and Hostel websites.
2. IIT Kanpur Hall Mess Menus - Hall websites and student portal references.
3. General PG & Tiffin Service Menus (North India/Delhi NCR region).
4. Typical weekly meal plan templates for North Indian households.

## Note on Usage
These sources were used to extract recurring Indian vegetarian dishes, identify seasonal trends, and understand standard meal patterns (e.g., Dal + Sabzi + Rice + Roti for Lunch). No single menu was copied wholesale.
